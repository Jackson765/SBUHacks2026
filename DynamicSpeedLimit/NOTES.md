### What the model does
- Loads the METR-LA CSV.
- Builds a sliding-window time-series feature set from recent traffic history.
- Trains a LightGBM regressor to predict speed.
- Trains a LightGBM classifier to predict congestion.
- Saves both models for reuse in inference.

### How inference works
- `predict_next_step(history_window)` loads the saved models.

- It returns:
  - predicted speed in mph
  - congestion probability between 0 and 1
