Regular playback: original video at 1× speed.

Regular playback uses the video's original frame rate at 1× speed. Analysis runs alongside it and can trail playback on slower hardware. Quick analysis processes as fast as possible and shows sampled detection images. A forecast needs 15 minutes of footage with valid speeds in every window. A weather advisory is automatically attempted after a forecast; weather is a current-conditions scenario, not historical footage weather. Calibration stays fixed during analysis.

Weather advisory

The entered base speed is a reference; recommendations may be higher, but never above the configured 70 mph legal maximum. Current weather is applied as a scenario to recorded footage. Unknown road types and severe weather require manual review. This experimental result is not an official speed limit.
